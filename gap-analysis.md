## C. Planned decisions whose underlying assumptions the research puts under pressure

These aren't changes to the locked rules. They are points where the research suggests an implicit assumption behind a §26 decision may not hold.

1. **"One active booking per customer"** assumes a customer can be reliably identified. Fresha identifies online customers through a
   verified account (phone SMS code, email, Google or Apple), and its staff-side client records have no mandatory unique field. If the plan's **no SMS
   OTP** rule also covers customers (the brief lists it beside the staff-auth rules without saying), the identity rule behind this decision
   needs an explicit answer (owner decision B-6). Evidence: `evidence/booking/public-09-after-time-continue.png`
2. **"Simple walk-in flow"** assumes the barber is known automatically. In Fresha the barber depends on how the sale is started (quick-sale lines
   default to the logged-in user). Evidence: `evidence/walk-in/quick-sale-02-haircut.png`
3. **"Cash + KBZPay"** assumes that recording the method is enough. In Fresha KBZPay carries no reference, and reconciliation is a manual count at
   register close. Evidence: `evidence/finance/register-close-01.png`
4. **"Stock transfers"** implicitly assumes a single movement. Fresha shows a two-step lifecycle (ordered → received, partial receipt), with stock
   leaving the source only at receipt. Evidence: `evidence/inventory/stock-transfer-T1-receive.png`
5. **"Multi-branch staff"** assumes schedules won't collide. Fresha auto-created identical shifts at both branches for a two-branch barber and
   allowed a cross-branch double booking. Evidence: `evidence/schedule/scheduled-shifts-baber-shop.png`, `evidence/booking/cross-branch-conflict-before-save.png`
6. **"In-app staff notifications"** assumes staff learn about changes made by others. Fresha appears not to notify the person who acted
   (INFERRED), and offers per-location and "only my appointments" filters that multi-branch staff may need.
7. **Revenue and commission dates**: our plan doesn't state whether they follow the service date or the payment date. Fresha uses the checkout date,
   which moved an early-paid appointment into an earlier pay period. Evidence: `evidence/payments/checkout-07-complete.png`
